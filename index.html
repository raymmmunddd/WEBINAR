04 · Two kinds of websites

Static vs Dynamic Sites

Flip the switch to compare how each one serves a page.

Static Dynamic

Static website

Made of pages written in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Every visitor gets the same files.

Great for: portfolios, landing pages, event sites.

Fun fact: the site you're on right now is static.

Dynamic website

Content lives in a database or a backend CMS. The server builds each page from that data.

Great for: online shops, social feeds, school portals.

Your Facebook feed looks different from your friend's. That's dynamic.

    07 · Talk to AI clearly

    Proper Prompting

    A strong prompt has four parts. Here they are, building a navbar.

    01

    Role

    Who should the AI be?

    "Act as a senior frontend developer."

    02

    Context

    What's the situation?

    "I am building a responsive navigation bar in HTML/CSS."

    03

    Task

    What exactly should it do?

    "Create a clean layout with a logo on the left and links on the right."

    04

    Constraints

    What are the rules?

    "Use Flexbox and do not use external libraries."
